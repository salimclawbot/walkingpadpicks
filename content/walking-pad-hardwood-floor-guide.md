## Comparison criteria

Use pass, fail and unknown rather than a blended score. One failed mandatory limit ends the comparison.

### Machine footprint

Measure every contact point and the full moving envelope, including feet, wheels and a folding joint. Compare it with the clear floor area rather than the room's wall-to-wall dimensions. Photograph the existing finish before installation so later marks are not confused with earlier damage.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Mat material

Use only a mat material permitted by both the flooring maker and machine maker. Rubber, PVC, foam and composite mats can interact differently with finishes and underfloor heating. Require published dimensions and a flat, stable surface; a yoga mat is not automatically equipment-rated.

### Floor finish

Identify solid timber, engineered wood, laminate or wood-look vinyl and find the finish maker's care limits. Avoid guessing from appearance. Adhesives, plasticizers, trapped moisture and abrasive grit can discolor or scratch a finish even when the treadmill itself never touches the boards.

### Caster design

Check whether transport wheels carry the machine only while tilted or also rest on the floor during operation. Small hard wheels concentrate load and can catch grit. Never drag the unit; follow the exact lifting and rolling procedure and protect thresholds along the travel path.

## Protect the floor as a complete system

The relevant system includes the finish, subfloor, mat, treadmill feet, moving belt, desk and travel path. A product advertised for hardwood can still be unsuitable for a particular lacquer, oil finish or heated floor. Start with the flooring documentation and the machine manual, then look for a mat that satisfies both.

### Make a floor record

Clean only as the floor maker directs and photograph the area in even light. Mark existing dents, gaps and color changes. Measure the machine's operating contact points and cut a paper template for the proposed mat. The mat should lie fully flat without crossing a board transition, floor vent or door route.

If the floor maker warns against a material or long-term impermeable covering, that instruction controls. Ask the mat manufacturer for composition rather than relying on words such as hardwood safe. Keep the answer, model and date. Unknown chemistry is a reason to pause, especially for a rental or newly refinished surface.

### Manage grit, heat and moisture

Hard particles trapped under a mat can become an abrasive layer. Inspect the underside using the permitted cleaning schedule and let every surface dry before reassembly. Do not use an unapproved solvent, polish or adhesive. If the machine has ventilation below the deck, preserve the specified air space instead of choosing a softer, thicker mat by intuition.

### Test movement without claiming soundproofing

Place reference tape at the mat corners and machine feet. After ordinary sessions, check for creep, curling, indentation, discoloration and new noise. Ask a downstairs neighbor or colleague to describe whether transmitted sound changed when that is relevant. The result applies only to that room, structure and setup.

### Rental and warranty boundary

Get permission before placing heavy powered equipment where a lease, strata rule or workplace policy may restrict it. Save the machine, mat and flooring care instructions. If either maker refuses compatibility or the floor shows a new mark, stop the trial and restore the area using approved methods rather than adding another layer.

A safety search is a dated check, not a lifetime guarantee. Search the exact brand and model in the [CPSC recall database](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls), review current [CPSC warnings](https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings), and check [SaferProducts.gov](https://www.saferproducts.gov/) before purchase and periodically after it arrives. On April 16, 2026, CPSC warned consumers to stop using specified Sperax Pro, Q1, RM-01 and RM-02 walking pads and treadmills because of reported fall, burn and fire hazards. Those models are excluded from this guide. Follow any stop-use instruction immediately.

## Hardwood-specific edge cases

Sunlight can change the colour of exposed boards while a covered rectangle changes more slowly. Before leaving a mat in one place for months, ask the flooring maker about light exposure and permissible coverings. Rotate or remove a mat only when both product instructions allow it; do not promise that a routine will prevent colour differences.

Board movement across seasons can create a ridge that was absent during installation. Recheck whether the mat remains flat and whether the machine rocks after humidity changes. Heated floors add another variable: obtain written temperature and covering guidance from the flooring supplier and verify that the treadmill maker permits that surface. Do not place powered equipment over a heating zone based on guesswork.

Apartment floors need two separate observations: visible finish condition and sound experienced elsewhere in the structure. A thicker layer may protect against scuffs yet make the treadmill less stable or restrict airflow. Prefer the thinnest documented system that passes both makers' requirements, then inspect at a defined interval. A new stain, soft patch, curl, rocking motion or heat buildup is a stop signal, not a reason to add another material.

### Resolve conflicts conservatively

When two official records disagree, do not select the value that makes a candidate look better. Save both records, contact the responsible manufacturer, and mark the field unknown until reconciled. The same rule applies when a marketplace title combines several generations or the manual lacks the retailer's claimed feature. Unknown is a useful decision result: it prevents a purchase from resting on an assumption.

### Evaluate the exact offer last

Only after a model passes identity, documentation, capacity, fit and current safety checks should the buyer inspect an offer. Confirm that the offer is for the same variant used in the worksheet, then note seller, shipper, availability, included parts and return terms. Price changes quickly, so this guide does not publish a static figure. An affiliate link is only a shopping path and never evidence that a model passed the checks.

### Keep the first trial reversible

Preserve packaging and follow the seller's trial conditions. Change one material variable at a time, use the machine only as the exact manual directs, and record pass, conditional, fail or unknown after ordinary use. Stop for unexpected acceleration or stopping, visible damage, unusual heat, smoke, burning odor, electrical problems, instability, a published warning, or any condition the manual identifies. Do not open the housing, bypass a safeguard or apply generic maintenance advice; contact the manufacturer or an appropriately qualified service provider.
