## Comparison criteria

Use pass, fail and unknown rather than a blended score. One failed mandatory limit ends the comparison.

### Deck height

Measure the highest occupied standing surface, not the shell beside it. Add only the footwear actually used. If the maker publishes several height figures, reconcile them to a diagram; never choose the smallest number because it makes the desk appear compatible.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Desk minimum height

Measure from finished floor to the working surface at its lowest usable setting, then confirm the desk can rise enough for the user standing on the deck. Include any controller, crossbar or drawer that reduces clearance beneath the desktop.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Shoe clearance

Check the actual shoe sole, toe profile and room to step on and off without striking the desk structure. Barefoot measurements do not represent use in shoes. Follow the machine's footwear instructions and keep laces and loose clothing away from moving parts.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Cable route

Map the power lead at every desk height and machine position. Use only the electrical arrangement permitted by the manual; do not place extension connections under the machine or across an entry path. The cable must not tighten when the desk moves.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Entry path

Reserve a repeatable route to approach, mount, stop and leave the machine. A low desk can hide controls or trap the user between legs and drawers. The configuration fails if a chair, cable or wall must be climbed over during normal changeover.

**Pass:** the fact is tied to the exact model and the planned setup. **Fail:** it conflicts with the manual, environment or a current stop-use notice. **Unknown:** the responsible source does not resolve it; do not buy on that claim.

### Storage height

Measure the exact storage gap at its narrowest point and include plugs, rails, incline feet and wheel housings. Confirm the manufacturer allows horizontal or vertical storage as planned. A nominal under-sofa gap is not enough when the travel path includes a lower lip.

## Calculate the real height budget

A low-clearance desk problem has two separate limits: whether the machine physically fits beneath the frame and whether the work surface can reach the user after the deck raises them. Draw a side elevation with floor, deck surface, shoe sole, elbow, desktop, crossbar and monitor. Record values in one unit and add a measurement tolerance rather than treating catalogue dimensions as exact construction drawings.

### Measure in operating position

Set the desk on a level floor and measure its working surface at the intended standing setting. Measure underside obstacles separately. A crossbar can clear the treadmill shell while colliding with a handrail hinge or the user's shoe. If a desk memory preset is used, verify it after the machine is in place and ensure the moving desktop cannot crush the monitor, shelf or cable.

OSHA's [workstation checklist](https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/checklists/evaluation) can help evaluate reach and display position. It does not certify desk-walking, prescribe speed or establish that a setup is safe for a particular person. Anyone with balance, mobility or medical concerns should seek individual professional advice before using powered walking equipment.

### Create a cable-state diagram

Photograph the cable route with the desk low, at walking height and at its highest setting. The treadmill plug should reach the specified outlet arrangement without being pinched by a desk foot or pulled by height changes. Keep power and control leads outside the mounting route and follow both manufacturers' instructions.

### Rehearse changeover while stopped

With power disconnected as directed, rehearse moving the chair, positioning the machine, setting the desk and storing the displaced item. The routine should not require unsupported lifting, dragging over flooring or reaching beneath powered equipment. Assign a storage place for the remote so the stop control is not missing at the next session.

### Pass/fail worksheet

Require passes for operating height, underside clearance, desk range, cable slack, entry route, reachable stop control and permitted storage orientation. Unknown counts as not ready. Optional app features, display style or color cannot compensate for a failed physical boundary.

A safety search is a dated check, not a lifetime guarantee. Search the exact brand and model in the [CPSC recall database](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls), review current [CPSC warnings](https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings), and check [SaferProducts.gov](https://www.saferproducts.gov/) before purchase and periodically after it arrives. On April 16, 2026, CPSC warned consumers to stop using specified Sperax Pro, Q1, RM-01 and RM-02 walking pads and treadmills because of reported fall, burn and fire hazards. Those models are excluded from this guide. Follow any stop-use instruction immediately.

## Low-clearance edge cases

A desktop measurement can be correct while a keyboard tray fails. Extend every tray, drawer and control paddle and map its lowest point. Monitor arms and clamp plates can also reduce usable space beneath the rear edge. Repeat the measurement with the desk at each saved height; memory buttons can be overwritten or selected accidentally.

Footwear changes the height budget and the way a user steps onto a short deck. Record the actual shoe, not an assumed sole thickness, and follow the treadmill maker's footwear requirement. If two people share the workstation, create separate desk presets only after each configuration passes. A setting that fits the taller person must not become the default for everyone.

Storage beneath a sofa or bed often fails at the entry lip rather than the main cavity. Measure the full route, including carpet pile, skirting, furniture feet and the angle needed to engage transport wheels. If movement requires lifting more than a user or workplace procedure permits, choose a different storage plan. Never store vertically unless the exact manual explicitly allows that orientation and explains how to secure the unit.

## Build an evidence workbook before shopping

Use one row per exact candidate, not per brand family. Capture the full model name, model or part number, regional voltage, selected colour or bundle when it changes components, manufacturer page, manual, current seller, return deadline and the date each source was checked. Screenshots should include the variant selector. A search-result snippet or review is a discovery aid, not sufficient evidence for a material limit.

Keep manufacturer facts, retailer facts and observations in separate columns. Manufacturer facts include documented dimensions, capacity, controls, warnings and permitted operating states. Retailer facts include the currently selected variant, seller, shipper, delivery estimate and return route. Observations include whether the machine fits the measured room and whether the planned handoff works. Never rewrite an observation as a universal product property.

### Resolve conflicts conservatively

When two official records disagree, do not select the value that makes a candidate look better. Save both records, contact the responsible manufacturer, and mark the field unknown until reconciled. The same rule applies when a marketplace title combines several generations or the manual lacks the retailer's claimed feature. Unknown is a useful decision result: it prevents a purchase from resting on an assumption.

### Evaluate the exact offer last

Only after a model passes identity, documentation, capacity, fit and current safety checks should the buyer inspect an offer. Confirm that the offer is for the same variant used in the worksheet, then note seller, shipper, availability, included parts and return terms. Price changes quickly, so this guide does not publish a static figure. An affiliate link is only a shopping path and never evidence that a model passed the checks.

### Keep the first trial reversible

Preserve packaging and follow the seller's trial conditions. Change one material variable at a time, use the machine only as the exact manual directs, and record pass, conditional, fail or unknown after ordinary use. Stop for unexpected acceleration or stopping, visible damage, unusual heat, smoke, burning odor, electrical problems, instability, a published warning, or any condition the manual identifies. Do not open the housing, bypass a safeguard or apply generic maintenance advice; contact the manufacturer or an appropriately qualified service provider.

### Update policy

Recheck links and safety status when the article is materially revised and immediately when a regulator or manufacturer publishes a relevant notice. Product names, manuals, apps, sellers and availability can change without warning. A dated audit makes that uncertainty visible and gives the next reviewer a reproducible starting point.
